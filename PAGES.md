# Available Pages

This POEMs prototype includes the following pages:

- done [My Funds](http://localhost:3000/my-funds) - Fund management and tracking
- [Unifund Registry](http://localhost:3000/unifund-registry) - Registry of unifunds with search and metrics
- [This Morning's Routes](http://localhost:3000/mornings-routes) - Morning delivery routes
- [Transition Dashboard](http://localhost:3000/transition-dashboard) - Transition metrics and analytics
- [Select Interventions](http://localhost:3000/select-interventions) - Browse and select interventions
